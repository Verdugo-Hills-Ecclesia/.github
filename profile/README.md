# Verdugo Hills Ecclesia

The GitHub home for the Verdugo Hills Christadelphian Ecclesia - a community of believers in the Los Angeles area seeking to follow the teachings of the Bible and share our faith.

## What lives here

- Website and member portal for the Verdugo Hills Ecclesia
- Documents and resources for Ecclesia members
- Community service and outreach projects
- Tools for managing Ecclesia functions
